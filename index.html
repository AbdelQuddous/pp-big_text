Privacy Policy

ShoutBoard — LED Banner

Last updated: September 8, 2026

ShoutBoard ("the App") is a free utility that turns your phone into a fullscreen readable sign — scrolling LED banners, big text, QR codes, playlists and exports. The App contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store on remote servers, or transmit any personal data. Everything you create stays on your device.

  • No personal information is collected
  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No tracking technologies
  • No advertising
  • No accounts or sign-in

Permissions

The App requests only the permissions required for features you explicitly use:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — used only when you tap voice dictation. Speech recognition is performed by your device's operating-system speech service; the App does not record, store, or transmit audio itself.
  • Media library / storage — used only when you export a banner as an image, GIF or video, or when you pick a file to import. Files are written to public media folders (Pictures/ShoutBoard, Movies/ShoutBoard) only on your explicit export action.

Data Stored Locally

The App stores the following on your device only:

  • Saved banners, playlists and message history
  • Themes, colors and display settings
  • Home-screen widget and Quick Settings tile content
  • Backup files you export yourself

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Sharing

The App can receive text or images shared to it from other apps, and can share banners you create via the operating system's share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share it.

Third-Party Services

The App uses no third-party services or SDKs. No Firebase, no Google Analytics, no ad networks, no external servers.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and collects no data from children or adults.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
